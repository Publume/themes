# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive, and harassment-free community for
everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

- Be constructive, specific, and kind.
- Discuss ideas and evidence rather than attacking people.
- Respect different experience levels and communication styles.
- Accept feedback and correct mistakes openly.
- Protect private information and responsible security reports.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, deliberate intimidation,
doxing, sustained disruption, and publication of private information are not
accepted in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to `publume.cloud@gmail.com`. Maintainers may
remove content, restrict participation, or ban contributors when necessary. They
will handle reports as confidentially and fairly as practical.

This code applies to repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, events,
and public representation of the Publume project.
